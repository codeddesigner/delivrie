Where Delivrie wins

Lower price at 20,000 and 100,000 emails a month, twice Resend’s free allowance, half the overage rate, and you pay in Naira through Paystack instead of a dollar card.

Where others are ahead

Resend keeps email content for 30 days (we keep it 1 to 7) and is slightly cheaper around 50,000 emails a month. Brevo adds SMS, WhatsApp and a CRM, and its free plan allows 300 emails a day.
