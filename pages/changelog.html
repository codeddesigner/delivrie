Nov 13, 2026

SMTP relay on ports 587 and 465

SMTPSending
  • Send from WordPress, Laravel, Django, Ghost, Supabase Auth and anything else that speaks SMTP: host smtp.delivrie.io, username delivrie, an API key as the password.
  • Port 587 with STARTTLS and port 465 with SSL/TLS, plus 2587 and 2465 for hosts that block the usual ports.
  • SMTP mail gets the same limits, logs, webhooks and suppressions as the API. Tag messages with an X-Delivrie-Tags header.
Nov 13, 2026

Support chat

Dashboard
  • Chat with the Delivrie team from any page in the dashboard. Replies arrive in the chat and by email.
Nov 13, 2026

Automations and Inbox

AutomationsInboxAPI
  • Automations send emails when an event arrives, a contact joins a segment, or on a schedule, with waits, conditions and branches.
  • Custom events API: POST /api/events with an event name, email and data.
  • Inbox: received email grouped into threads, with labels, archive and replies that keep Message-ID threading.
  • Runs show every step with timestamps; retry or skip a failed step after fixing the cause.

Nov 12, 2026

New pricing, credits and billing

Billing
  • Pro includes 20,000 emails a month and Scale 100,000, with yearly plans at ten months’ price.
  • Paid plans keep sending past their allowance and pay overage per 1,000 at month end. Set a spending cap in Billing.
  • Pay-as-you-go credits from ₦5,000 or $5 that never expire, and a marketing add-on priced by contacts.
Nov 12, 2026

Test addresses and natural-language scheduling

API
  • Send to delivered@, bounced@ and complained@test.delivrie.io to test events without touching your reputation.
  • scheduled_at accepts phrases like “in 1 hour” and “tomorrow at 9am”.
