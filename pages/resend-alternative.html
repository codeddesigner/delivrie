Why switch

Why teams move from Resend to Delivrie

Half the price

$10 a month for 20,000 emails instead of $20, and $0.45 per extra 1,000 instead of $0.90. At 100,000 emails a month you pay $45 instead of $65.

Same API shape

POST /emails with from, to, subject, html, text, cc, bcc, reply_to, headers, attachments, tags, template and an Idempotency-Key header. The response is { id }.

A bigger free plan

6,000 emails a month and 200 a day, enough to launch a real product before you pay anything.

Dollars or Naira

Pay by card in US dollars, or in Naira through Paystack with local cards and no exchange-rate surprises.

Where Resend is still a good fit. Resend keeps the full body of sent emails for 30 days; Delivrie keeps it for 1 to 7 days depending on plan, while recipients, statuses and every event stay so logs and metrics keep working. Resend also renders React Email components in its Node SDK; with Delivrie you send the rendered HTML.

FAQ

Delivrie vs Resend: common questions

Is Delivrie cheaper than Resend?

Yes. Pro is $10 a month for 20,000 emails (Resend Pro is $20), extra emails are $0.40 to $0.45 per 1,000 (Resend charges $0.90), and the free plan sends 6,000 a month instead of 3,000.

Is Delivrie a good Resend alternative?

If you send transactional email through an API or SMTP, yes: the request format matches Resend’s /emails endpoint, and Delivrie adds broadcasts, automations, a shared inbox and an MCP server for AI agents on every plan.

Can I switch from Resend without rewriting my code?

For most REST integrations, yes. Change the base URL to https://delivrie.io/api and use a Delivrie API key. Verify your sending domain in Delivrie first, and update your webhook handler if you use one.

Is Delivrie better than Resend?

It’s cheaper, has a larger free plan and lets you pay in Naira. Resend keeps email content longer (30 days) and renders React Email in its Node SDK. Pick the one that fits how you send.

Does Delivrie work for teams in the US?

Yes. Prices are in US dollars, you can pay by card, and the API, SMTP relay and MCP server work from anywhere.
