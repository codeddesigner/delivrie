01Infrastructure

Delivrie runs on SOC 2 audited cloud infrastructure in the EU (Ireland). The providers we use are listed on our Subprocessors page. Production access is limited to the people who run the service and protected with two-factor authentication.

02Application security

  • Every change is reviewed and deployed from version control.
  • Webhooks are signed so you can verify they came from us.
  • Email authentication (SPF, DKIM and DMARC) is set up for every domain you add.
  • Addresses that bounce or complain are suppressed automatically to protect your reputation.

03Compliance

We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the GDPR, as described in our Privacy Policy and DPA. We do not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification yet. Email security@delivrie.io for our answers to your security questionnaire.

04Responsible disclosure

Email security@delivrie.io with steps to reproduce. We reply within 2 business days, will not take legal action for good-faith research, and credit researchers who want it. Please do not access other customers’ data or run denial-of-service tests.

Questions about this policy?Email security@delivrie.io or write to South Circle, Lagos, Nigeria.
