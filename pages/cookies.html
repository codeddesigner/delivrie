Cookie Policy

What Delivrie stores in your browser on delivrie.io and in the dashboard, and how to control it.

Last updated 13 November 2026Effective 1 December 2026

01What we use

We keep browser storage to a minimum and use no advertising or analytics cookies. Emails you send can include open and click tracking, which you control per domain; that is covered by the Privacy Policy, not this page.

02What we store

NamePurposeTypeKept
sb-…-auth-tokenKeeps you signed in to the dashboardEssentialUntil you log out
dlv_teamRemembers your last teamEssentialUntil cleared
dlv_currencyRemembers Naira or dollars on pricing and billingPreferenceUntil cleared
dlv_themeRemembers System, Light or Dark themePreferenceUntil cleared
dlv_docs_langRemembers your code language in the docsPreferenceUntil cleared

These are stored in your browser’s local storage rather than as cookies, so they are never sent to other sites.

03Your choices

  • Essential: needed for sign-in and security. Always on.
  • Preferences: theme, currency and layout choices. Clearing them resets the defaults.

You can clear this storage in your browser settings. Clearing essential items will sign you out.
