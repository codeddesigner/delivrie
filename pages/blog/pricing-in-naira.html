Pricing in Naira

Why Delivrie charges Nigerian teams in Naira, what you get on each plan, and what stays the same in dollars.

Delivrie teamNov 12, 2026 · 4 min read

Most email platforms bill in dollars. For a Lagos startup that means a price that moves with the exchange rate, a card that may not work for foreign payments, and a bill that grows faster than revenue. We wanted a price you can plan around.

One price list, two currencies

Every plan has a Naira price and a dollar price. Pro is ₦10,000 or $10 a month for 20,000 emails. Scale is ₦45,000 or $45 for 100,000. You choose the currency at checkout and pay with a local card through Paystack.

Pay for what you send

If a subscription doesn’t fit, buy credits instead. They start at ₦5,000 for 8,300 emails and never expire, which suits seasonal campaigns and low-volume OTPs.

On Pro and Scale we don’t stop your email when you pass the allowance. Extra emails are billed per 1,000 at the end of the month, and you can set a spending cap so there are no surprises.

What stays the same

The API, logs, webhooks and MCP server are identical on every plan and in both currencies. See the full breakdown on the pricing page.
