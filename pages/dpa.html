This addendum applies when Delivrie processes personal data on your behalf. It is part of the Terms of Service and needs no signature, but you can ask us for a signed copy for your records.

This summary is for convenience. The full text below is what applies.

01Scope and roles

This DPA covers personal data in Customer Data that Delivrie processes to provide the service. You are the controller (or a processor acting for your own controller) and Delivrie is the processor (or subprocessor).

02Details of processing

ItemDescription
Subject matterSending, receiving and tracking email
DurationFor the term of the agreement
Data subjectsYour recipients, contacts and end users
Data typesEmail addresses, names, message content, attachments, engagement events, IP addresses
Special categoriesNone intended. Do not send health or similar data unless agreed in writing

03Our obligations

  • Process personal data only on your documented instructions, including those given through the API and dashboard.
  • Make sure anyone with access is bound by confidentiality.
  • Apply the technical and organisational measures described on our Security page.
  • Help you respond to data subject requests and carry out impact assessments.
  • Delete or return personal data when the agreement ends.

04Subprocessors

You authorise the subprocessors listed on our Subprocessors page. We will give 30 days notice of any new subprocessor by email and on that page. If you object on reasonable data-protection grounds and we cannot address it, you may terminate the affected service with a pro-rata refund.

05International transfers

Transfers out of Nigeria, the EEA, the UK or Switzerland are covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2 and 3), the UK Addendum and the transfer requirements of the Nigeria Data Protection Act, which are incorporated by reference.

06Personal data breaches

We will notify you without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting your personal data, with the information you need to meet your own reporting duties.

07Audits

We will answer reasonable security questionnaires and share our security documentation on request. You may audit us once a year with 30 days notice, at your cost, if that documentation does not answer your reasonable questions.
