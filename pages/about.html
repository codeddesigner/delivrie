Our story

Why we built it

Teams across Africa kept hitting the same wall: emails that never arrived, dashboards that hid the reason, and pricing in dollars that punished growth.

So we built the email platform we wanted. A clean API, honest logs, templates and an MCP server for agents, priced in Naira with dollars for everyone else.

We are a small team in Lagos, and we answer support ourselves.
