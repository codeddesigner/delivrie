Usage

Do and don’t

Do

  • Use the logo on Ink or Paper backgrounds
  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the mark
  • Write the name as Delivrie, capital D

Don’t

  • Stretch, rotate, outline or recolour the logo
  • Place it on busy photos or low-contrast yellow
  • Combine it with another logo into a new mark
