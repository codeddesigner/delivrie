FAQ

Delivrie vs Brevo: common questions

Is Delivrie cheaper than Brevo?

For email, yes: Pro is $10 a month for 20,000 emails (Brevo Starter is $32), Scale is $45 for 100,000 (Brevo Starter is $82), and there’s no paid add-on to remove a provider footer.

Is Delivrie a good Brevo alternative?

If you mainly send transactional email and newsletters from your product, yes. If you rely on Brevo’s SMS, WhatsApp or CRM, Brevo still covers more.

Does Delivrie add a footer to my emails?

No. Delivrie doesn’t add its own branding to your transactional emails on any plan.

Can I send newsletters with Delivrie?

Yes. Broadcasts are free up to 1,000 contacts, with segments, topics and an unsubscribe page included.
