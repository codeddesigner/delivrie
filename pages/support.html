Common questions

I connected Delivrie to ChatGPT or Claude. How do I disconnect it?

In Delivrie, open Settings, then Connected apps, and revoke the app. You can also remove it from the AI app's connector settings.

Why can't the assistant send from my address?

Delivrie only sends from domains you have verified in Domains. Add your domain, publish the DNS records, then try again.

Which plan am I on and how much have I used?

Open Billing in the dashboard, or ask your assistant to show your Delivrie usage.
